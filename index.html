AI Expense Tracker - Privacy Policy

What this app does

AI Expense Tracker helps you see where your money goes. It finds bank and card transaction alerts on your phone, adds up what you spent and received, and lets you organise payments into categories and groups.

What the app reads, and why

SMS messages (permission: Read SMS). The app reads the SMS on your phone to find messages about money moving in or out of your bank account or card: the amount, the date, the other party's name and the last digits of your account or card. This is the main thing the app does. Messages that are not about a payment, such as one-time passwords, offers and personal chats, are ignored and are not kept.

Contacts (permission: Read contacts, optional). Some UPI payments only show a phone number. If you allow it, the app looks up that number among the contacts saved on this phone, only to show the saved name instead of the number. The app does not copy your contact list: it keeps only the name of the matching contact beside that payment, on your phone, and never sends it anywhere. You can say no and the app still works; those payments then show the number.

What you add yourself. Payments you enter by hand, notes, groups, labels, categories and any picture you choose for a group are stored in the app.

Where your information stays

Everything above is processed and stored on your phone, inside the app. The app has no accounts and no server. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your messages, contacts or payment details, and the app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking.

Backup

If Google backup is on in your phone's settings, Android saves this app's data, including the payments stored in it, to your own Google account. It restores it if you reinstall the app from Google Play or set up a new phone. Android and Google do this, not the app, and it is covered by Google's terms and privacy policy. You can turn it off in Android Settings, Google, Backup.

Reporting a wrong payment

Report a wrong payment, inside a payment's details, prepares a short text with the message (amounts, account digits, numbers, ids and links removed) and opens Android's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself. You can edit the text first.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can withdraw SMS or contacts access at any time in Android settings. In the app, Settings, Delete all transactions removes the payments it has stored. Uninstalling the app removes its data from your phone. A copy kept by Android backup stays in your Google account until you delete it there.

Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage on your phone and is protected by your phone's own lock and encryption. If you lose your phone, use your phone's lock and remote-wipe tools.

Children

The app is meant for adults managing their own money. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts to handle information differently, this policy will be updated first and the date below changed.
